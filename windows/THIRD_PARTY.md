`vendor_argos_bpe.py` is the unmodified MIT-licensed BPE implementation from
argosopentech/argos-translate, Git blob `2d9fde9145e9748a1b5e7e289cdb2afc788ff1af`.
The copyright and MIT license are retained at the top of the source and copied
into the translation component's licenses folder. This supports the official
Spanish-to-English language pack's BPE tokenizer without installing Stanza/Torch.
Source: https://github.com/argosopentech/argos-translate/blob/master/argostranslate/apply_bpe.py
